# Scrolly 1.0 📜

The first full version of Scrolly. The old prototype (0.1) needed Python and a terminal; this is a complete
rebuild that you download, double-click, and use. Free, offline, and yours: nothing leaves your computer.

## Download

- **Windows 10/11:** `Scrolly-1.0.0-windows.zip`
- **Mac (Apple Silicon, M1 or newer):** `Scrolly-1.0.0-macos-arm64.zip`

The app is not code-signed, so the first launch shows a warning. Windows: **More info → Run anyway**. Mac:
**System Settings → Privacy & Security → Open Anyway**. Full steps are in the
[README](https://github.com/Amartain/scrolly#download-and-run).

Coming from the prototype? **Settings → Data → Restore Scroll** reads your old JSON export.

## What's new since the prototype

**A whole new look**
- Two hand-made worlds: **Serene Peak**, an ink painting in green and paper, and **Hidden Pavilion**, a bold
  lacquer city in teal or crimson. Each has its own day, golden hour and dusk.
- Lacquer panels with gold trim, pixel lettering, and a choice of reading font (Atkinson Hyperlegible or
  OpenDyslexic).
- A living mountain in the header, with a little cultivator who climbs to meditate while you work.

**Planning that feels like a ritual**
- Empty your mind onto the scroll, estimate the journey, weigh your energy (optional), then seal the scroll.
- A Day Summary shows how much light is left before your sunset, without ever turning red.

**Doing, one thing at a time**
- The Immediate Trial up front, with a timer that survives reloads, sleep and closed tabs.
- **Seclusion**, a focus room on the mountain: an incense timer, Harmonious Cycles (pomodoro-style) or River
  Flow (rest you earn as you work), plus a side panel for a parallel task, a sudden trial and loose notes.
- Small celebrations: a seal stamped on every conquest, and a ceremony when the day is done.

**Gentle on purpose**
- No streaks, no "overdue", no red badges, no numbers that go down. Ordinary tasks you didn't reach fade away at
  4 AM; the ones that truly matter return to the Horizon.
- **The Golden Core** (optional): an energy ledger for people who overcommit, which reminds you that rest
  comes first.

**Looking back**
- Momentum and Rhythm charts that only ever rise, the Grand Archives of every past day, and your time in
  Seclusion kept as Bamboo Scraps.

**Learn by playing**
- **Knowledge of the Dao**, a short tour that teaches Scrolly as you use it. Finish it and see what happens.

**Everything else**
- Discord Dispatch: your day as Discord markdown, ready to paste.
- Backups as plain CSV files you can open in any spreadsheet.
- Every action reachable by keyboard; reduced-motion support throughout.

And a few secrets. You'll know them when you find them. 🤫
